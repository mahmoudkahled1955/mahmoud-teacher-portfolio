How I design a lesson

The same four ideas run through all my e-books and games.

1

Start simple

One small idea at a time, in plain words.

2

Show it

Diagrams, animations and 3D visuals, not only text.

3

Let them try

Predict, change a setting, see what happens.

4

Check understanding

Worked examples, quizzes, hints and cheat sheets.
